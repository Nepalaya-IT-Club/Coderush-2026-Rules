# Code of Conduct — Behavior on College Premises

This document outlines the rules of conduct that every participant of **CodeRush 2026** must follow while on Nepalaya College premises during the event. By registering for the event, all participants and team members agree to these terms.

---

## 1.1 General Conduct

- Participants must remain within the designated event area (halls, labs, or rooms assigned for CodeRush) for the duration of the hackathon.
- Respect for fellow participants, mentors, judges, volunteers, and college staff is mandatory at all times.
- Any form of harassment, discrimination, or disrespectful behavior toward another participant or organizer will not be tolerated and may result in disqualification.
- College property, furniture, and equipment must be used responsibly. Any damage caused by a participant or team will be the responsibility of that team.
- Participants must carry their college/participant ID at all times during the event.

## 1.2 Overnight Conduct (24-Hour Event)

- Since the event runs overnight, participants must stay within the designated sleeping/rest areas during rest periods and must not wander into restricted parts of the college premises.
- Noise levels must be kept reasonable during late-night hours out of respect for nearby rooms and any residential areas.
- No participant may leave the event premises and return without informing a volunteer or organizer, for safety and headcount purposes.
- Consumption of alcohol, tobacco, or any prohibited substances on college premises is strictly forbidden and will result in immediate disqualification and further disciplinary action.

## 1.3 Team Conduct

- Each team must stay within their allotted seating/work area unless moving for food, breaks, or mentor sessions.
- Only registered team members are allowed to work on the project. External help beyond permitted mentorship is not allowed.
- Teams must submit their project before the announced deadline. Late submissions will not be accepted or judged.
- Any team found using pre-built projects, plagiarized code, or work done before the event start time will be disqualified.

## 1.4 Organizer's Authority

- Organizers and volunteers reserve the right to issue warnings or disqualify any participant/team that violates these rules.
- In case of an emergency, participants must immediately inform the nearest volunteer or organizer.
